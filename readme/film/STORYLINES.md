# Spare demo film — storylines

The one thing: whatever you copied earlier is one keystroke and a few letters away.
Spine: one viewer gets back one clip they copied 40 copies ago, and pastes it where they were typing.
Facts used on screen: ⌘⇧V, word search in any order, Return pastes into the app you came from, ⌘1–⌘9,
⌘P pin, ⌘F filter, ⌘⌫ then ⌘Z, hold ⌘ for hints, password-manager copies never recorded,
18–22 MB in memory and ≤ 0.042% CPU at rest (X11), no network.

## A. The one you lost (recommended)

- Device / form: a moment in someone's day, as one continuous take.
- What you see: a developer mid-reply in Messages needs the API key they copied an hour ago; the
  camera never cuts, it pushes from the desktop into the palette, through the search, and back
  out to the message as the key lands.
- First 2 s: a half-written message, "here's the staging key:", cursor blinking; above it a ticker of
  the last 40 copies flying past the menu bar too fast to read.
- Beats: hook (the need) → tension (the key scrolls away under 40 newer copies) → ⌘⇧V, palette
  blooms out of the cursor → type "stag key", rows fold to one → Return, it lands in the message →
  rapid proof: ⌘P pin, ⌘F images, ⌘⌫/⌘Z, hold ⌘ → claim "Everything you copied, one keystroke away"
  → icon end card, `⌘⇧V`.
- Look: macOS Tahoe desktop, the real Liquid Glass palette at 1.8×, violet #5546F0 rationed to the
  selection, the payoff and the end card. SF Pro.
- Sound: a warm mid-tempo beat; key clicks, a soft glass "bloom" on open, a tick per filtered row, a
  thock on paste.

## B. Cut on the beat

- Device / form: type carries the argument, cut on the beat.
- What you see: giant keycaps fill the frame and are struck on the beat; every chord cuts to the
  palette doing exactly that one thing, then back to type.
- First 2 s: ⌘ ⇧ V as three 600 px keycaps, pressed in sequence on the first three beats; the third
  press is the cut into the palette.
- Beats: ⌘⇧V → "type anything" (search folds the list) → ⌘1…⌘9 → ⌘P → ⌘F → ⌘⌫ ⌘Z → the numbers land
  as type (20 MB · 0.04% CPU · 0 network) → end card.
- Look: flat violet and white colour fields swapping every half beat, keycaps as the one material.
- Sound: punchy 120 bpm track, every keycap a hit on the beat.

## C. The stack (bold)

- Device / form: the object is the explanation, as match cuts and an endless zoom.
- What you see: the icon's three glass cards become the palette's rows; each copy anywhere on the
  Mac throws a new card onto the stack, and the camera dives into a card to land in the app it
  came from.
- First 2 s: the icon fills the frame, the front card slides out and turns out to be a real clip.
- Beats: icon → cards fan into the palette → zoom into a row = the app it was copied from → copy
  throws a card back → search shuffles the stack to one → paste → cards collapse back into the icon.
- Look: deep violet ground from the icon's gradient, frosted glass cards, white type.
- Sound: airy synth bed, paper-and-glass card whooshes, one bass hit on the final collapse.
